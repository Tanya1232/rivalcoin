KENNEY
https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-protagonists
Player: characterMedium with skaterMaleA skin. Idle, jump and run were converted
from the original FBX files. The walk adapts the run timing and stride. The aim
and shoot clips are original poses on the same rig. Model, texture and clips
are packaged in player.glb.

https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
Sports sedan and crate. Original sedan-sports and box models, packaged with
their original texture atlases embedded.

https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
Construction barrier with its original texture atlas embedded.
